Muon spectra from daemonflux
Quantity : muflux  (location = generic)
Columns  : E_GeV = total muon energy, p_GeVc = total momentum,
           zen_XXdeg = dPhi/dE at that zenith angle
Units    : dPhi/dE in cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1 GeV^-1 (raw, NOT multiplied by E^3)
Errors   : 1-sigma, same units, in muon_dphidE_errors.csv
